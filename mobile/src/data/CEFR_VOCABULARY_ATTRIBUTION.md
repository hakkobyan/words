# CEFR vocabulary data attribution

The generated `cefrVocabulary.*.ts` files contain 1,000 English headwords for each level A1–C2 and Russian translations.

## Level data

- A1–B2 entries are based primarily on **The CEFR-J Wordlist Version 1.5**, compiled by Yukio Tono, Tono Laboratory, Tokyo University of Foreign Studies. The dataset permits research and commercial use without charge when cited. Source: <https://github.com/openlanguageprofiles/olp-en-cefrj>
- C1–C2 entries are based primarily on the **Octanove Vocabulary Profile C1/C2 Version 1.0**, created by Octanove Labs and distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>
- Gaps left after normalization, de-duplication, part-of-speech checks, and preservation of the application's earlier vocabulary were filled from **Words CEFR Dataset**, Copyright (c) 2024 Belikov Maxim, distributed under the MIT License: <https://github.com/Maximax67/Words-CEFR-Dataset>

## Russian translations

Russian translations are adapted from **FreeDict English–Russian 2025.11.23**, maintained by Karl Bartel and generated by WikDict from Wiktionary data via DBnary. The dictionary is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported License: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/>

Source dictionary: <https://download.freedict.org/generated/eng-rus/>

When FreeDict offered several meanings, the selection was cross-checked against the **word2word** English–Russian bilingual lexicon by Yo Joong Choe, Kyubyong Park, and Dongwoo Kim. The project is distributed under the Apache License 2.0 and its precomputed lexicons were built from OpenSubtitles2018: <https://github.com/kakaobrain/word2word>
